Upload your .docx or .doc file, select PDF as the output, and click Convert. FileForge preserves your formatting and delivers a clean PDF instantly.

Can I convert JPG, PNG or images to PDF?

Yes! FileForge supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP, GIF, and TIFF to PDF conversion. You can also convert between image formats — PNG to JPG, WEBP to PNG and more.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

The short version: We do not store your files. Files are deleted immediately after conversion. We use Google AdSense to serve ads, which may use cookies. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time.

1. Who We Are

FileForge ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at fileforge.app. We provide a free online file conversion service. For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@fileforge.app.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Files You Upload

When you upload a file for conversion, it is temporarily stored on our server for the sole purpose of performing the conversion. Files are permanently deleted immediately after your download is delivered, and in all cases within 1 hour of upload. We do not read, analyse, copy, or share your file contents.

2b. Log Data

Our server automatically records standard access logs when you visit the site, including:

  • Your IP address (anonymised after 24 hours)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time of visit
  • Referring website
  • File type converted (not file contents)

This data is used solely for security monitoring and aggregate analytics (e.g. understanding which conversion types are most popular).

  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

FileForge uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website and other sites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by:
